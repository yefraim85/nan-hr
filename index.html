English level needed

Describe the real level the job needs

Use these practical examples to tell us exactly how much English the role requires.

Basic English

Level 1

Understands simple words and short questions. e.g. “What is your name?”, “Where are you from?”, “Please wait here.”

Simple conversation

Level 2

Talks with customers about basic daily things: greeting guests, explaining prices, simple directions and basic service questions.

Driving / directions

Level 3

Understands and explains locations, routes, pickup points and timing. e.g. “Turn left after the hotel”, “I will pick you up at 3 PM”, “The road is closed.”

Hotel reception / service

Level 4

Speaks politely with guests, explains services, handles simple complaints, check-in/check-out, bookings and requests.

Technical explanation

Level 5

Explains work processes, products, problems or instructions in detail — how something works, reporting an issue, or giving clear instructions.

Fluent English

Level 6

Speaks naturally and confidently with foreign clients, handles complex situations, negotiates and solves problems in English.

The process

Eight clear steps from brief to hire

You always know what happens next — and you only pay the rest when you decide to hire.

1

We send you a full questionnaire about the position.

2

After you complete it, we may ask more questions to fully understand the role and the person you want.

3

Once the position is clear, you pay the 10% upfront deposit and we start looking for candidates.

4

We screen candidates and run the initial interviews ourselves.

5

We send you the relevant candidates, and you run the secondary interviews directly.

6

In some cases we may join the secondary interview — or join if you ask us to. It depends on the role.

7

After the interviews you decide: hire a candidate, reject, or keep interviewing more options.

8

You only pay the remaining payment when you decide to hire.

Replacement & refund

A policy that protects you

If the employee does not stay for at least two weeks, we refund the 90% payment. Here's how every situation is handled.

Situation Refund result
We do not find an employee 10% deposit refunded
No good options for the employer 10% deposit refunded
Employee does not stay 2 weeks 90% payment refunded
Client changes their mind or cancels (reason unrelated to candidate quality) 10% deposit not refunded
Disagreement on candidate quality Discussed fairly — if unresolved, 50% of deposit refunded (client pays only 5%)

These rules describe the basic engagement structure and can be adjusted by written agreement when needed.
